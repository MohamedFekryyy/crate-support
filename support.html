Support

Crate is a native macOS sample browser. We can help with library access, indexing, auditioning and getting sounds into your DAW.

Get started

On first launch, choose Everywhere and select your startup disk in the macOS picker, or choose individual folders. Change this later in Library Sources. Protected folders and external drives may need separate access.

Contact us

Open a support issue with your macOS version, Crate version, what you expected, and what happened. Please avoid posting private sample names or audio. The issue tracker is public.

Open a support issue

What Crate stores

Crate keeps its index and preferences on your Mac. It does not change your sample files. Read our privacy policy for details.
