Support

Help with library access, indexing, auditioning and getting sounds into your DAW.

Get started

On first launch, choose This Mac, click Allow Access and select your startup disk in the macOS picker. Or choose Specific folders and pick only the folders you want. Change this later in File › Library Sources. Protected folders and external drives may need separate access.

Contact

Open a support issue with your macOS version, Crate version, what you expected and what happened. The issue tracker is public, so leave out private sample names and audio.

What Crate stores

Crate keeps its index and preferences on your Mac and never changes your sample files. See the privacy policy.
