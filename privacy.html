Privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026

Crate is a macOS app for browsing and auditioning audio files you choose to make available on your Mac.

Your library stays on your Mac

Crate reads the folders you select, or the locations you grant access to when choosing This Mac. It stores an index, audio analysis results, favorites, listening history and preferences locally on your Mac so search and playback work quickly. Crate does not modify your sample files or write metadata into sample folders. Cloud-only files are not downloaded by Crate.

No account or tracking

Crate does not require an account and does not collect or transmit your audio, filenames, library index, listening history or personal information to CPL OF MNGS. There are no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs or tracking in the app.

Your choices

You can remove a folder from Crate in the app. You can remove Crate's local data in ~/Library/Application Support/Crate and its settings from macOS. Removing the app does not remove your original audio files.

Support requests

If you contact us for support, the information you choose to send will be used to answer your request. Do not share audio files or private sample names in a public issue. Contact support with privacy questions.
